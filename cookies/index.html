Cookie policy.

What cookies this site uses, who sets them, and how to change your preferences.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They let the site recognise your device, remember your preferences and collect information about how you interact with it. Similar technologies such as local storage, session storage and web beacons may be used for comparable purposes, and references to cookies here include them.

2. Who sets cookies on this site

  • Claim 3000 Ltd The data controller, responsible for the overall cookie strategy and the legal basis for cookie use.
  • Top Offer Italia Ltd The technology platform operator, deploying and managing cookies on Claim 3000 Ltd’s behalf.
  • Third parties Analytics, advertising and tracking providers, where their tags are placed on the site.

3. The categories we use

Only the strictly necessary category is set automatically. Everything else waits for you to accept it.

Strictly necessary

Session management, such as remembering your progress through the claim form, security tokens and infrastructure cookies. Legal basis: legitimate interests and technical necessity. No consent is required under UK PECR.

Analytics and performance

Tools such as Google Analytics and Hotjar, or equivalents, may be used to understand which pages are visited, where visitors leave the form and how the site performs across devices. This information is used in aggregate and does not identify individuals. Legal basis: consent, asked for on first visit. Retention: up to 26 months for analytics data; raw session recordings deleted within 90 days.

Marketing and tracking

Tags such as the Meta Pixel, Google Ads conversion tracking, the TikTok Pixel and affiliate or referral tracking may be used to show relevant advertising and measure campaigns. They are only placed if you accept marketing cookies. Retention: typically 30 to 180 days depending on the platform.

Functional

Remembering choices you make, such as your progress in the claim form or your cookie preferences. Legal basis: legitimate interests where solely for user experience, or consent where linked to third-party features.

4. Third-party policies

Cookies placed by third-party services are governed by their own policies: Google, Meta, Hotjar.

5. Managing your preferences

You can set your browser to notify you when a cookie is placed, or to block third-party cookies. Guidance for each browser is at allaboutcookies.org. You can opt out of Google Analytics across all websites with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on, and manage advertising cookies at youronlinechoices.com.

Refusing analytics or marketing cookies does not stop you using this site or submitting a claim. Only the strictly necessary cookies are needed for the claim form to work.

6. How this sits with the privacy notice

This policy covers what is stored on your device. What we do with the personal data you give us, who it is shared with, how long it is kept and what rights you have over it are set out in our privacy notice.

7. Contact

For any question about cookies or about the personal data behind them: Claim 3000 Ltd, Unit 31, Cariocca Business Park, Hellidon Close, Ardwick, Manchester, M12 4AH. contact@ladclaims.com. 0800 024 8904.

If you believe this site has set cookies without asking you first, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk, which regulates cookies in the UK under PECR.
