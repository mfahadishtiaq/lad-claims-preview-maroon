Had car finance with BMW Financial Services?

If commission or the lender relationship was not explained clearly, the agreement may need to be reviewed. Checking takes about a minute and costs nothing.

Check my claim
01

Why people check BMW Financial Services agreements

BMW Financial Services is one of the lenders people most often name when they start a check. That is because most people remember the dealership, not the finance company behind the agreement.

The FCA has described three arrangements that may give grounds to complain: a discretionary commission arrangement, where the broker could adjust your interest rate and earn more as a result; unusually high commission that was not adequately explained; and a tie to one lender presented as though the market had been compared.

None of this says BMW Financial Services did anything wrong. Each agreement is assessed on its own facts by the lender, under the applicable rules.

02

What happens next

The dates the FCA has published, so you know where things stand.

  1. 01

    Your agreements are traced

    A soft credit search looks for motor finance in your name. It is not visible to lenders and does not affect your credit score.

  2. 02

    A letter goes to the lender

    If you appoint the partner firm, they contact the lender and put the complaint to them on your behalf.

  3. 03

    The lender responds

    Under the FCA scheme, lenders were expected to begin assessing from 30 June 2026 for agreements taken out from 1 April 2014, and from 31 August 2026 for earlier ones.

  4. 04

    Where the scheme stands

    Parts of the scheme are currently suspended by the Upper Tribunal, so timing is uncertain. Anyone not contacted by their lender can still complain to the firm by 31 August 2027. The FCA estimates an average of around £829 per eligible agreement, though nothing is guaranteed.

Source: the Financial Conduct Authority. Read the FCA’s own statement.

03

Not sure which lender you had?

You do not need to know. The search looks across multiple credit reference agencies for agreements between 2007 and 2024.
