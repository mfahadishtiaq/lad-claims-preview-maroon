Privacy notice.

How Claim 3000 Ltd, trading as LAD Claims, handles your personal data.

1. Who we are

Claim 3000 Ltd, trading as LAD Claims, is the data controller for the personal data described in this notice. Company number 09371796, registered in England and Wales. Authorised and regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority, reference 833604. Registered address: Unit 31, Cariocca Business Park, Hellidon Close, Ardwick, Manchester, M12 4AH. Our ICO registration number is ZA534924.

The website is operated on behalf of Claim 3000 Ltd by Top Offer Italia Ltd, which provides the digital infrastructure powering this site and its forms as our technology and marketing data processor, under a data processing agreement.

2. What we collect

When you use the claim form, or contact us, we collect:

  • Your full name, title and date of birth
  • Your email address and phone number
  • Your current address and any previous addresses you give us
  • Details of vehicle finance agreements found or given, such as the lender, the type of agreement and the approximate year
  • Your answers to eligibility questions
  • Your electronic signature
  • Your marketing preferences

Where analytics run on the site, we also receive anonymised technical data about how visitors use it, as described in the cookies section below.

3. Why we use it

  • To find your agreements. With your consent, a soft credit search is carried out through Valid8 IP Ltd. A soft search is not visible to lenders and has no effect on your credit score.
  • To assess eligibility and, if you choose paid support, to pass your details and instruction to our partner law firm.
  • To communicate with you about your enquiry or claim.
  • To meet our regulatory obligations, including FCA record-keeping.
  • To improve the website, using anonymised analytics data to understand how visitors use it and how it performs.
  • For marketing, only where you have agreed to it, and you can withdraw that agreement at any time.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data. We may share it with:

  • Top Offer Italia Ltd, as our technology and marketing data processor, operating the website and form infrastructure under a data processing agreement.
  • Valid8 IP Ltd, for the soft credit search only, to identify your finance agreements.
  • Our partner law firm, when submitting or progressing your claim.
  • The Financial Ombudsman Service or the FCA, if required to escalate or report on a complaint or claim.
  • Legal and professional advisers, where necessary for the conduct of your claim or our regulatory obligations.
  • IT and cloud service providers, including secure form submission, CRM and communication tools, all bound by data processing agreements.

Some of our data processors may operate outside the UK or European Economic Area. Where this occurs, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, such as UK-approved Standard Contractual Clauses or adequacy decisions, to protect your data to the standard required under UK GDPR.

5. How long we keep it

  • Claims: for the duration of your claim plus 6 years after resolution, as required by FCA rules and limitation periods.
  • Unsuccessful enquiries and cases that do not proceed: up to 12 months from your last interaction, then securely deleted.
  • Marketing consent: until you withdraw consent, plus a reasonable record-keeping period.
  • Analytics: retained in aggregated, anonymised form; raw session data deleted within 26 months.

6. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access: request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectification: ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erasure: ask us to delete your data where there is no lawful reason to retain it.
  • Restriction: ask us to restrict processing in certain circumstances.
  • Portability: receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Objection: object to processing based on legitimate interests or for direct marketing.
  • Withdraw consent: at any time where processing is based on consent.

To exercise any of these rights, email contact@ladclaims.com or write to us at our registered address. We will respond within 30 days. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

7. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration or destruction. These include encrypted data transmission, access controls and regular security reviews. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children

Our services are not directed at persons under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has submitted data to us, please contact us so we can delete it.

9. Cookies

This site uses cookies and similar technologies. The strictly necessary ones are needed for the claim form to work. Analytics and marketing cookies are only set if you accept them, and refusing them does not stop you using the site.

Which cookies are set, who sets them, the legal basis for each category, how long each is kept and how to change your preferences are all set out in our cookie policy.

10. Contact

For any privacy query, data subject request or complaint: Claim 3000 Ltd, Unit 31, Cariocca Business Park, Hellidon Close, Ardwick, Manchester, M12 4AH. contact@ladclaims.com. 0800 024 8904.
