2. Our service

We check whether you may have been mis-sold a PCP (Personal Contract Purchase) or HP (Hire Purchase) car finance agreement because of undisclosed or discretionary commission arrangements between the lender and the broker or dealer.

What we do is identify your finance agreements and assess your eligibility. We do not handle claims. If you decide to go ahead, we refer you to the partner law firm, and it is that firm that prepares and submits the complaint to your lender and, where appropriate, escalates it to the Financial Ombudsman Service or the FCA redress scheme. You appoint that firm, not us. See section 4.

You do not need to use a claims management company. You can complain directly to your lender, and then to the Financial Ombudsman Service, at no cost. We do not charge you at any stage. The partner firm’s service is fee-bearing; see section 6. The free routes are set out on Claiming for free.

3. Eligibility

To use our service you must:

  • Be 18 or over.
  • Have held a PCP or HP car finance agreement with a UK-regulated lender.
  • Not already have an active claim or legal proceedings in relation to the same agreement.
  • Have the authority to act on behalf of any joint agreement holders, where applicable.

A soft credit search, which is not visible to lenders, may be used to locate your finance agreements. This will not affect your credit score.

4. Letter of Authority and your instructions

By signing and submitting the Letter of Authority, you appoint BlueLion Claims, a trading name of BlueLion Law Limited, regulated by the Solicitors Regulation Authority under number 815166, as your authorised representative to act on your behalf in relation to your car finance claim. You do not appoint Claim 3000 Ltd. Your electronic signature has the same legal effect as a handwritten signature.

You must provide accurate and complete information. Providing false or misleading information may result in termination of your claim and potential liability for costs incurred. You may withdraw your Letter of Authority at any time; see section 8 for the financial implications of doing so after the cooling-off period.

Under that firm’s terms you also agree to:

  • Notify them of any changes to your contact details or personal circumstances relevant to your claim.
  • Not independently approach the lender, the Financial Ombudsman Service or any third party about the same claim without notifying them first.
  • Inform them immediately if you receive any correspondence from your lender or the Financial Ombudsman Service about your claim.
  • Not accept any settlement offer from a lender without first consulting them.

5. The free initial check

There are no upfront fees. The initial eligibility check is free. It involves a soft credit search to identify PCP and HP finance agreements registered against your name and address history. This check is not visible to lenders and does not affect your credit score or credit file. Any fees will be clearly communicated before you sign the Letter of Authority.

6. Fees

If a claim goes ahead, the partner law firm works on a no win no fee basis and charges 18% to 36% including VAT of any compensation recovered. If the claim is settled through litigation the maximum deduction is capped at 48% including VAT. You pay nothing if your claim is unsuccessful.

The exact percentage depends on the value of your award:

AwardFee
Up to £1,49936% including VAT
£1,500 to £9,99933.6% including VAT
£10,000 or above18% including VAT

No win, no fee: you pay nothing if your claim is unsuccessful. All fees are subject to the FCA’s cap on CMC charges.

Litigation is only recommended where it is believed you would achieve a better outcome than through the FCA’s redress scheme. If a case proceeds to litigation, fees may be charged on a different basis, such as an hourly rate plus a success fee, and some fees may be recoverable from the defendant if the claim succeeds. If litigation is unsuccessful no fees are payable, although there may be adverse costs, including the defendant’s legal costs and disbursements. These can be covered by After the Event insurance, subject to policy terms, which would likely be recommended if your claim proceeds to litigation.

You have a 14-day cooling-off period from the date you sign your Letter of Authority. During this period you may cancel without charge by emailing contact@ladclaims.com, calling 0800 024 8904, or writing to our registered address. We will not commence substantive work during the 14-day period without your express consent.

After the 14-day period you may still cancel at any time. If work has already been carried out on your claim, a termination fee may apply at a rate of £80 plus VAT per hour for work completed to that point, with a written breakdown of the work undertaken.
