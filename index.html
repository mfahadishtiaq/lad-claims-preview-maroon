01

Were you mis-sold car finance?

01The date

Financed between 2007 and 2024

You may be eligible to make a claim if you entered into a PCP or hire purchase agreement for a car, van, motorbike or campervan between 6 April 2007 and 1 November 2024.

02The arrangement

Commission was part of the deal

Your lender paid commission to the dealer or broker that arranged your car finance agreement. The FCA looks at three arrangements in particular: a discretionary commission arrangement, where the dealer could raise your interest rate; commission that was high for the amount you borrowed; and a tie to one lender presented as a search of the market.

03The disclosure

You may not have been told everything

The commission, its potential impact on your finance, or the relationship between the dealer and lender may not have been clearly explained to you.

04The paperwork

Start with what you remember

You do not need the original paperwork, lender’s name or exact dates to get started. Provide the details you remember and our partners can help trace your agreements.

These are general indicators only and do not confirm eligibility. Your lender will assess your agreement against the FCA scheme rules and any applicable exclusions.

Leasing is not covered. Personal Contract Hire is excluded from the FCA scheme. If you leased the vehicle rather than having an option to buy it at the end, the agreement falls outside this.

How it works

  1. 01

    You tell us what you remember

    A postcode and a few details is enough to begin. You do not need the original paperwork, the lender’s name or the exact dates.

  2. 02

    We search, and show you what we found

    A soft credit search locates the motor finance agreements linked to you, including any held under a former name or a previous address. It is not visible to lenders and does not affect your credit score.

    You review what was found and the routes open to you, including the free ones. If you choose paid support, your claim is passed to a solicitor, and instructing them is your decision. You are free to choose a different firm.

04

Information disclosure

About your choices

You can make this claim without a claims management company, and you never have to use one. Going direct means putting the complaint to your lender yourself and, if their answer does not satisfy you, passing it to the Financial Ombudsman Service. Neither route costs anything.

The FCA has also set up an industry-wide redress scheme covering eligible motor finance agreements. Parts of it are currently suspended, so when the scheme opens, and when any payment would follow, is not yet settled.

Where we act, we aim to recover the most the evidence in your case supports. We cannot promise that is more than you would recover going direct yourself.

Where the scheme stands

The Upper Tribunal has suspended parts of the scheme, on terms agreed with the four firms that challenged it. Firms must still comply with every rule that is not suspended, and the routes direct to lenders and to the Financial Ombudsman Service stay open throughout.

Start dates and payment timings are not settled. We track what the FCA publishes and update this page when the position moves. Read the FCA’s own statement.

Why us

We find your agreements

Our agreement-tracing system checks major credit reference agencies and available vehicle records to identify historic car finance agreements linked to you, including those held under a former name or previous address.

We bring the available details together, saving you from searching through old paperwork or contacting every lender yourself.

Our legal partners do the hard work

With your claim eligibility established, our trusted legal partners take over the hard work. They review each agreement, gather supporting evidence, prepare and submit your claim, communicate directly with your lender, manage every response and challenge any offer that does not fairly reflect the evidence.

Our legal partners work on your behalf to build the strongest claim supported by the facts and pursue the best compensation available under the applicable rules.

Our legal partners get paid when you do

You pay nothing upfront. Our legal partners deduct their agreed fee only from compensation recovered after a successful claim. Transparency is central to how we work, so every fee is explained clearly before your claim begins. Clear terms from the start mean you can move forward with confidence.
