Claim 3000 Ltd does not handle claims itself. Claims are handled by a partner law firm, and we are paid a fee by that firm for the referral as part of an ongoing marketing agreement. If you choose the paid service, fees of 18% to 36% including VAT apply if your claim succeeds, capped at 48% including VAT where a claim is settled through litigation. You can cancel without charge within 14 days of instruction. You can complain to your lender yourself for free, or refer a complaint to the Financial Ombudsman Service.
